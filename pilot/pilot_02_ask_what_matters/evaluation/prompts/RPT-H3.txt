3. Water: don't depend on a filter alone everywhere

A Sawyer Squeeze, Katadyn BeFree or Platypus QuickDraw is an excellent backpacking filter, but it's important to understand what portable filters can and can't do.

The CDC recommends boiling as the most reliable treatment. Where boiling isn't practical, its next-preferred approach is filtering followed by disinfection when contamination is uncertain. Many portable microfilters remove parasites and bacteria but do not reliably remove viruses.

9. Where to spend versus where to save

Save on: cooking pot, spoon, fleece, hiking shirts, stuff sacks, simple gloves, basic trekking poles and some camp accessories. You don't need $100 titanium cookware to safely boil water.

Rent initially: technical mountaineering boots, crampons, ice axe, helmet, harness and group rope/rescue equipment. After taking a course and learning what type of mountaineering you actually enjoy, you'll know whether your future is mostly snow climbs, glacier travel, alpine rock or technical ice—and those require substantially different equipment.
