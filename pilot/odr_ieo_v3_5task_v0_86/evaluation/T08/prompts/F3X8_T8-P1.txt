# Destination assessments

## 1. Sanur, Bali

### July weather and seasonality

Two historical datasets put July daytime temperatures around **28°C**, nighttime temperatures around **24–25°C**, and sea temperature near **27°C**. Monthly rainfall is approximately **53–54 mm**. Reported humidity is **73–80%**, while sunshine ranges from **248 to 307 hours** [1][2].

The [Denpasar July climate page](https://weather-and-climate.com/Denpasar-July-averages) uses 1976–2025 averages and classifies July among Bali’s better-weather months [2]. A second Copernicus-derived source, updated October 30, 2025, reports a maximum UV index of **11**, which is extreme [1].

The reported 2 versus 18 rain days should be read as a definition issue. In practical terms, July usually has:

- Many dry or mostly dry days.
- Occasional brief showers.
- Strong tropical sun even when the air feels breezy.
- Southeast trade winds and possible choppy conditions outside protected water.
- No guarantee against heavy rain, lightning, rough-sea warnings or flight disruption.

Bali lies outside the principal western North Pacific typhoon tracks affecting Hainan and Vietnam. It can nevertheless experience strong wind, coastal swell and disruptions from regional weather or volcanic ash.

**July verdict:** genuinely one of the best months for Bali, but also one of the busiest and most expensive.

### Sea and beach safety

Sanur’s offshore reef usually makes the nearshore water less energetic than exposed beaches such as Kuta, Canggu or Uluwatu. It is not risk-free:

- At low tide the water can become extremely shallow, limiting swimming.
- Coral fragments, rocks, sea grass and occasional marine life make water shoes useful.
- Boat channels can have stronger current and traffic.
- Public lifeguard coverage is not continuous along every section.
- “Calm-looking” water is not a substitute for checking tides, flags and local advice.

For children aged five and eight:

- Choose the beach section directly in front of a reputable resort or staffed beach club.
- Keep the five-year-old within arm’s reach.
- Use properly fitted buoyancy aids, not inflatable toys as safety devices.
- Do not allow swimming around jetties or boat channels.
- Use the pool when the tide, wind, water quality or supervision is unsuitable.

### Transport from Shanghai

Likely patterns include:

- **Nonstop PVG–DPS when scheduled:** approximately 6–7 hours in the air.
- **One-stop service:** commonly via Hong Kong, Guangzhou, Xiamen, Kuala Lumpur, Singapore or another regional hub; total scheduled journey can exceed 10–14 hours.

Indicative central-Shanghai-to-Sanur time:

- Home to PVG: 1–1.5 hours.
- International check-in and security: allow about 2.5–3 hours.
- Flight: 6–7 hours nonstop.
- Immigration, baggage and customs: 45–90 minutes, sometimes longer in peak periods.
- Airport to Sanur: ordinarily 30–60 minutes, but congestion can push it beyond an hour.

**Realistic door-to-door total:** approximately **10–12 hours nonstop** or **14–18 hours with a connection**.

Use a prearranged hotel transfer or licensed airport transport. Grab and Gojek are useful locally, but pickup points and vehicle availability can vary. Ordinary cars may not carry child seats; request appropriate restraints from a private transfer company in advance. Avoid scooters with children.

### Entry and passport issues

Eligibility must be checked for each passport on the official [Indonesian eVisa portal](https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id/) [10]. Depending on nationality, travelers may qualify for visa-free admission, an e-visa, visa on arrival or may need advance approval.

Common conditions for visa-on-arrival-eligible visitors have included:

- Passport validity of at least six months at entry.
- An onward or return ticket.
- A 30-day stay, sometimes extendable once.
- A fee around IDR500,000 per person, approximately ¥220–240 depending on exchange rates.

These are not universal entitlements. Children normally require their own passports and entry authorization.

Bali also applies a foreign-tourist levy, commonly IDR150,000 per person, through the official [Love Bali portal](https://lovebali.baliprov.go.id/) [11]. Verify exemptions and the exact amount for the actual travel date.

Anyone resident in China on a foreign passport must also confirm that leaving China will not invalidate their Chinese visa or residence status and that they possess the right documents to re-enter.

### Accommodation

Sanur is preferable to Kuta for this family because it is quieter, easier to walk, less focused on nightlife and has a calmer shoreline. Central or south Sanur offers the best balance of beach access and dining. North Sanur can be quieter but less convenient for some family facilities.

Do not assume that a “family room” permits two adults plus two children. Enter both children’s ages when booking and obtain written confirmation covering:

- Maximum occupancy.
- Bed arrangement.
- Extra-bed charge.

### Dining and everyday supplies

Sanur has local Indonesian food, pizza, pasta, burgers, Japanese food, seafood and café breakfasts. Child-friendly choices include:

- Nasi goreng or mie goreng with mild seasoning.
- Chicken satay with sauce on the side.
- Grilled chicken or fish with rice.
- Soup, eggs, pancakes, toast and fresh fruit.
- Pizza and pasta for familiar-food evenings.

Indicative costs for four:

- Simple local warung meal: **¥140–280**.
- Comfortable casual restaurant: **¥300–550**.
- Resort or premium beachfront restaurant: **¥600–1,000+**.
- Groceries, fruit, snacks and water for a week: **¥350–700**, depending on imported products.

Central Sanur has supermarkets and numerous Circle K, Indomaret and similar convenience stores. Use sealed or properly treated water for drinking and tooth brushing. Choose busy restaurants with good food turnover; ensure meat and seafood are thoroughly cooked. Ice is generally lower risk in reputable hotels and established restaurants than at improvised stalls.

### Medical care and emergencies

Sanur has pharmacies and outpatient clinics; more advanced private care is available in the Denpasar area. [Bali International Hospital](https://baliinternationalhospital.com/) [18] is a relevant Sanur-area provider, but current specialties, pediatric coverage, insurance billing and emergency capability should be confirmed before travel.

Common numbers to save and reconfirm with the hotel:

- **112:** general emergency where locally active.
- **110:** police.
- **118/119:** ambulance or medical emergency services, depending on local routing.

Response and English-language capability can vary, so the hotel front desk and travel insurer’s assistance line may be the fastest practical coordinators.

Insurance should cover:

- Private hospitalization.
- Pediatric treatment.
- Medical evacuation.
- Trip interruption and weather disruption.
- Baggage delay.
- Water activities that will actually be undertaken.
- Any pre-existing conditions disclosed as required.

Many policies exclude scooters, unlicensed driving, high-risk water sports, intoxication and known storms occurring before insurance was purchased.

### Activities

Good family options include:

- Sanur beach, pool and promenade cycling.
- Short outrigger or glass-bottom boat outing only in suitable weather and with child lifejackets.
- [Bali Bird Park](https://www.bali-bird-park.com/) [15].
- A short temple or craft-village visit.
- A family cooking activity.
- [Waterbom Bali](https://www.waterbom-bali.com/) [14], with adults supervising separately where rides have age or height restrictions.

## 2. Da Nang and the Hoi An coast, Vietnam

- Warm nights around **25–26°C**.

- Heavy showers or thunderstorms despite strong sunshine.

**July verdict:** viable and good value, but only with strict heat management.

### Beach safety and crowds

My Khe and nearby beaches are long, sandy and convenient. They are also open-water beaches where waves and currents can change. Swim only:

- In marked zones.
- During staffed hours.
- When flags permit.
- Well away from fishing boats and drainage outlets.
- Before lightning or storm cells arrive.

Da Nang is a major domestic summer destination, so beachfront areas can be busy at sunrise and late afternoon. Midday may appear quieter because of the heat rather than because conditions are better.

### Transport

Transfers:

- Da Nang airport to My Khe/An Thuong: roughly 15–30 minutes.
- Airport to a resort south of the city: approximately 25–40 minutes.

Grab and metered taxis are convenient. For Hoi An or Ba Na Hills, a private car is easier with two children. Request child restraints in advance.

### Entry

Vietnam has offered electronic visas to a broad range of nationalities for stays of up to 90 days, with single- and multiple-entry choices, but that does not justify assuming eligibility for unknown passports.

Historically quoted government fees have been around US$25 for single entry and US$50 for multiple entry. Confirm:

- Passport eligibility.
- Required remaining validity.
- Exact legal name and passport number.
- Approved entry airport.
- Processing time.
- Whether a child needs an individual eVisa.
- Re-entry documentation for China.

Avoid third-party websites styled to look official.

### Accommodation, dining and activities

Best areas:

- **My Khe/An Thuong:** walkable dining and strong value, but more urban and potentially noisy.
- **Southern beachfront resort strip:** quieter and more self-contained, but greater reliance on taxis.
- **An Bang:** attractive for a relaxed village feel, but the longer airport transfer and variable road/sidewalk conditions reduce convenience.

Indicative nightly prices for four:

- Value apartment/family room: **¥500–1,000**.
- Comfortable resort or two-bedroom residence: **¥1,000–1,800**.
- Higher-comfort beachfront resort: **¥2,000–3,500+**.

Family meals generally cost:

- Comfortable restaurant: **¥250–500**.
- Premium resort meal: **¥550–1,000+**.

Suitable activities include My Khe beach, Hoi An’s old town in the late afternoon, the Cham Museum, a lantern craft, Ba Na Hills, swimming pools and light boat activities in safe conditions. Ba Na Hills can be much cooler—VietnamOnline cites roughly **17–20°C**—but is a long, busy and expensive outing [3]. Marble Mountains involve stairs and heat and are less suitable at midday.

Emergency numbers are commonly:

- **113:** police.
- **114:** fire.
- **115:** ambulance.

Travel insurance should include private treatment and evacuation. Use bottled or treated water, established restaurants and fully cooked seafood.

## 3. Sanya, Hainan

### July weather and seasonality

Sanya averages about **31°C by day and 27°C at night** in July. The gathered 1976–2025 dataset reports approximately **286 mm of rain over 12 days**, describing July as the wettest month [5]. The sea is typically very warm, approximately **29–30°C**, but warm water does not mean safe water.

Conditions can include:

- Intense sun and high heat index.
- Heavy late-day downpours.
- Thunderstorms.
- Tropical-cyclone watches and warnings.
- Rough surf, beach closures and flight disruption.

**July verdict:** possible for a resort-and-pool holiday, but not the best choice when the primary aim is reliable beach weather.

### Yalong Bay versus Haitang Bay

- **Yalong Bay** is the stronger beach choice for this family. It has a mature resort environment and usually offers more practical swimming access, subject to daily conditions.
- **Haitang Bay** has newer luxury resorts and major attractions but is known for stronger open-water conditions. Some sections or resorts restrict or discourage sea swimming. Choose Haitang mainly for the resort, pools, aquarium and water park—not on the assumption of safe daily ocean swimming.

Never enter the sea when a resort has closed its beach or raised warning flags.

### Transport and local logistics

Shanghai–Sanya nonstop flying time is normally around 3–3.5 hours when direct service is available.

Door-to-door estimate:

- Central Shanghai to departure airport: 1–1.5 hours.
- Domestic-airport allowance: 2–2.5 hours.
- Flight: 3–3.5 hours.
- Baggage and transfer: 1–1.5 hours.

**Total:** about **7–8.5 hours**.

Sanya Phoenix Airport to:

- Sanya Bay: about 20–35 minutes.
- Yalong Bay: about 35–55 minutes.
- Haitang Bay: about 45–75 minutes.

A resort transfer or ride-hailing car is easiest. Confirm child restraints if wanted.

For travelers already lawfully in China, there is no separate Hainan immigration process for an ordinary domestic trip. Foreign nationals should carry their passports, ensure the hotel can complete registration and check any travel-status questions with China’s [National Immigration Administration](https://en.nia.gov.cn/) [13].

### Accommodation and costs

Indicative July rates for a legal family-of-four configuration:

- Value hotel/apartment: **¥700–1,400 per night**.
- Comfortable Yalong Bay family resort: **¥1,400–2,600**.
- Higher-comfort suite or Haitang Bay luxury resort: **¥2,800–5,000+**.

Family resort dining can be expensive. Budget:

- Casual local meal: **¥180–350 for four**.
- Comfortable restaurant: **¥350–700**.
- Luxury-resort buffet or premium seafood: **¥700–1,300+**.

Activities include pools, water parks, aquariums, Nanshan cultural tourism, rainforest attractions and resort children’s clubs. Rainy-day alternatives are stronger in Haitang Bay, but travel between bays can be time-consuming.

Emergency numbers in China:

- **110:** police.
- **119:** fire.
- **120:** ambulance.

Yalong Bay has access to Sanya hospitals, while the Hainan branch of the PLA General Hospital is in the Haitang Bay area. Confirm insurance direct-billing arrangements; many facilities require payment first.

## 4. Qingdao

### July weather and seasonality

The gathered Qingdao source, updated October 30, 2025, reports:

- **30°C maximum, 25°C average and 20°C minimum**.
- **179 mm precipitation over nine rainy days**.
- **321 sunshine hours**.
- Maximum UV index of **11** [6].

Its 95% humidity figure likely uses a different measurement approach than the other destination datasets and should not be compared literally. The practical expectation is warm, humid coastal weather with possible fog, heavy rain and sunny spells.

The sea is generally around **23–25°C** as a planning estimate: swimmable for many people, but noticeably cooler than Bali, Da Nang or Sanya.

July risks include:

- Heavy showers and local flooding.
- Fog or low cloud.
- Typhoon remnants or coastal wind.
- Green-tide algae in some years.
- Jellyfish or other seasonal marine warnings.
- Extremely crowded bathing beaches.

**July verdict:** a reasonable beach-and-city trip, especially for avoiding international formalities, but not a reliably dry tropical holiday.

### Beach choice and safety

- **Shinan urban beaches:** convenient for city attractions but often very crowded.
- **Huangdao/Golden Beach:** more expansive and more resort-like, but farther from central-city attractions and the airport.

Use only officially open bathing beaches and stay inside marked swimming areas. Follow closure notices, lifeguard directions and jellyfish or water-quality advisories.

### Transport from Shanghai

Likely options:

- Frequent nonstop flights from Shanghai airports, around 1.5–2 hours in the air.
- High-speed rail from Shanghai Hongqiao, generally around 6–7.5 hours depending on service and destination station.

Estimated door-to-door:

- Air: **5–6.5 hours**.
- Rail: **8–9.5 hours**, but with less airport procedure and more freedom to move during the trip.

Qingdao Jiaodong Airport to Shinan can take roughly 45–75 minutes; Huangdao may take 60–100 minutes depending on traffic and route. Qingdao has useful metro coverage, though taxis or ride-hailing are easier with tired children and beach gear.

### Accommodation, dining and activities

Indicative nightly family costs:

- Value apartment or family room: **¥600–1,200**.
- Comfortable hotel or two-room arrangement: **¥1,200–2,200**.
- Higher-comfort seafront accommodation: **¥2,400–4,000+**.

Meals:

- Dumplings, noodles or home-style dishes: **¥120–250 for four**.
- Comfortable local seafood meal: **¥300–700**.
- Premium hotel or live-seafood dining: **¥700–1,200+**.

Suitable activities include beach time, Qingdao Aquarium, Polar Ocean World, the Olympic Sailing Center, coastal walks, a short Laoshan excursion and the Tsingtao Brewery Museum. In bad weather, aquarium and museum options are stronger than in a remote beach resort.

Emergency numbers are China’s **110, 119 and 120**. Qingdao has several major tertiary hospitals and generally the easiest medical and language logistics for a Shanghai-based domestic family.

# Recommended seven-day Sanur itinerary

## Trip assumptions

- **Seven calendar days and six hotel nights.**
- Day 1 is the outbound travel and arrival day.
- Day 7 is the return travel day.
- All six nights are in Sanur; there is no hotel change.
- The itinerary assumes a flight arriving by mid-afternoon and a return flight leaving in the late afternoon or evening.
- If actual flight times differ, remove activities rather than compressing them.
- Shanghai and Bali are both UTC+8, so no clock-change jet lag is expected. Travel fatigue still warrants a light first day.
- Daily cost estimates below cover local transport, meals and activities for four, **excluding flights and accommodation**.

## Day 1 — Shanghai to Sanur

### Morning

- Simple breakfast at home.
- Travel to PVG with a generous buffer.
- Keep one set of children’s clothes, swimwear, medication and basic toiletries in hand luggage.

### Afternoon

- Fly to Denpasar.
- Complete immigration, collect baggage and meet a prebooked driver.
- Transfer to Sanur, normally 30–60 minutes but potentially longer in traffic.
- Check in and inspect balcony locks, room configuration and pool access.

### Evening

- Early dinner near the hotel, ideally around Jalan Danau Tamblingan or the central Sanur beachfront.
- Choose mild nasi goreng, grilled chicken, noodles, soup or pizza.
- Ten-minute orientation walk only if the children still have energy.
- Bedtime close to the normal Shanghai schedule.

**Local cost:** ¥500–900.  
**Weather alternative:** none needed beyond using covered airport transport.  
**Do not add:** sunset temple visits or a second cross-island transfer.

# Health, safety and accessibility plan

## Sun and heat

Bali’s July maximum UV index is reported around **11**, Da Nang around **14**, and Qingdao around **11** [1][4][6]. For both children:

- Apply broad-spectrum SPF 50+ 20–30 minutes before exposure.
- Reapply after swimming and at least every two hours.
- Use long-sleeved rash guards, hats and sunglasses.
- Prioritize beach time before 10:00 and after 16:00.
- Carry water and oral rehydration salts.
- Stop activity for headache, unusual irritability, nausea, dizziness, cramps or reduced urination.

Never leave a child in a parked vehicle, even briefly.

## Water safety

- An adult should remain within arm’s reach of the five-year-old in the sea.
- Assign one supervising adult at a time; do not rely on general group awareness.
- Use certified flotation devices of the correct size.
- Check flags, tides and local warnings every day.
- Leave the water immediately for thunder or lightning.
- Do not swim after dark, around boats or in closed sections.
- Confirm lifeguard coverage; never assume a hotel beach is continuously guarded.
- Resort pools also require active adult supervision.

## Food, water and minor illness

Pack or know the local equivalent of:

- Children’s fever medication with dosing instructions.
- Thermometer.
- Oral rehydration salts.
- Plasters and antiseptic.
- Motion-sickness treatment after medical advice.
- Insect repellent.
- Any regular medicine in original packaging.
- Copies of prescriptions and insurance details.

Seek medical care promptly for breathing difficulty, persistent vomiting, dehydration, severe abdominal pain, blood in stool, high fever, head injury or a significant marine sting.

## Low-effort transport and accessibility

No mobility needs were specified, but a lower-effort design benefits a five-year-old:

- Use one hotel for all six nights.
- Prebook airport transfers.
- Use private cars for excursion days rather than multiple transport changes.
- Carry a compact folding stroller if the five-year-old still uses one when tired.
- Expect uneven pavements and occasional steps in Bali and Hoi An.
- Sanur’s promenade is relatively easy but not perfectly level or obstruction-free.
- Avoid scooters.
- Keep one completely flexible day.

# Final decision

For this family and an unspecified July, **Sanur offers the best balance of weather reliability, warm and relatively sheltered water, family accommodation, dining convenience, age-appropriate activities and adequate downtime**. Its higher transport cost is compensated by a substantially better seasonal fit than Sanya and more comfortable temperatures than Da Nang.

Choose a different destination if one of these priorities dominates:

- **Choose Qingdao** if avoiding international immigration, minimizing cost and shortening travel are most important.
- **Choose Da Nang** if a convenient nonstop fare is available and the family tolerates intense heat.
- **Choose Sanya** if the priority is a self-contained Chinese resort with pools and indoor attractions, and the family accepts that July beach conditions may be poor.

For Bali, the best-value version is not the cheapest room or the busiest itinerary. It is a **two-bedroom Sanur base, direct flights if affordable, two paid family attractions, two slow beach/pool days, one flexible weather day and protected midday rest every day**.
