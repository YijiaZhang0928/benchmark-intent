I plan to systematically study entrepreneurship over the next 1–2 years so that I can prepare for launching my own startup in the future. I already have substantial professional and management experience and an MBA-level business background.

Please compare degree and non-degree entrepreneurship programs, executive education, accelerator programs, online courses, mentoring/networking routes and practical venture-building options. Cover curriculum, time commitment, cost, access to networks/mentors, hands-on opportunities, fundraising/business-model/product/market/team-management learning, and the value of each route.

Then create a phased 1–2 year learning and experimentation plan that can coexist with my existing job and family responsibilities. Do not assume which education format or learning style I prefer when those choices would materially change the plan.
