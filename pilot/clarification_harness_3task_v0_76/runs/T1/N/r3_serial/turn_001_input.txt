I plan to apply for PhD programs in artificial intelligence abroad within the next 1–2 years. I am currently an undergraduate computer-science student with strong academic performance and an interest in AI, especially deep learning and NLP, but I have not yet decided on a specific research direction.

Please provide a comprehensive research-based comparison of suitable AI PhD directions and programs across major regions such as North America, Europe, and Asia. Cover research strengths, curriculum structures, academic resources, employment prospects, application requirements, language requirements, research/internship preparation, tuition, scholarship/funding mechanisms, and representative universities/programs.

Based on these objective constraints, create a phased preparation and application plan for the next 1–2 years. The plan should be specific enough to guide research preparation, school/program selection, application-material preparation, and timeline management. Do not assume unstated personal preferences when trade-offs would materially change the recommendation.
