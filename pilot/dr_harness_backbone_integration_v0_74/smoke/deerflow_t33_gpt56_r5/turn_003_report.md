# Recommendation for Xiao Bai

## Bottom line

For one Samoyed in a spacious Shanghai home, with cost, reliability, easy maintenance, and pet acceptance as priorities, I would buy in this order:

1. **A grooming vacuum system first**—better value than either a robot vacuum or professional-style dryer for controlling Samoyed shedding.
2. **A reputable complete dog food selected only after confirming Xiao Bai’s age, weight, body condition, and health status.**
3. **A Xiaomi/Mijia Smart Pet Feeder 2**, primarily for portion control and backup feeding—not as the sole caregiver.
4. **A Xiaomi/Mijia Smart Pet Fountain 2 plus a large stainless-steel backup bowl.**
5. **A Xiaomi or EZVIZ indoor pan/tilt camera using local microSD storage**, rather than an imported subscription-focused pet camera.
6. **One correctly sized KONG rubber food toy and one washable sniffing/foraging toy.**
7. Add a **Roborock P20 Pro-class robot vacuum later** only if the family values automatic floor cleaning enough to justify another roughly ¥2,300–4,000.
8. Buy a **high-velocity dryer only if the family regularly bathes Xiao Bai at home**.

A realistic core purchase budget is approximately **¥2,000–3,200**, including the first large bag of food but excluding a robot vacuum and dryer. Non-food consumables should be roughly **¥35–90 per month**; food is likely to dominate ongoing costs.

Prices below are **target checkout ranges**, not guaranteed quotations. Chinese e-commerce pricing changes significantly with coupons, government subsidies, 618, and 11.11 promotions.

## Important limitation: food remains provisional

Xiao Bai’s exact age, weight, body condition, allergies, and current food are unknown. Therefore, it would be unsafe to prescribe a precise food variant or daily gram amount.

Before buying food, have the family send:

- A photo of the current food label
- Xiao Bai’s weight
- Age and neuter status
- Whether stools, skin, ears, and appetite are currently normal
- Any veterinarian-diagnosed condition
- Body-condition score, ideally confirmed by a veterinarian

Do not choose food mainly by whether meat appears first in the ingredient list. WSAVA specifically warns that ingredient lists do not reveal ingredient quality and can be misleading; manufacturer nutrition expertise, formulation, quality control, feeding directions, and suitability for the individual dog are more useful criteria. WSAVA also does **not** “approve” particular brands. [citation:WSAVA Global Nutrition Guidelines](https://wsava.org/Global-Guidelines/Global-Nutrition-Guidelines/)

# 1. Cleaning-system comparison

These three systems solve different problems, so they should not be treated as substitutes.

| Option | Representative brands | Typical upfront target | Smart features | Convenience and maintenance | Likely acceptance | Value for this household |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| **Grooming vacuum** | Oneisall LM2/BM3; Neakasa P1/P2 | ¥450–900 | Usually little or no app functionality | Empty cup after grooming; periodically wash/replace filters; stores multiple brush attachments | Medium initially, potentially high after gradual noise training | **Best first purchase** |
| **Robot vacuum/mop** | Roborock P20 Pro; Dreame S/P series; ECOVACS T series | ¥2,300–5,000 | Mapping, rooms, schedules, no-go zones, obstacle avoidance, self-cleaning on selected models | Still requires brush checks, dock cleaning, bags, filters, mop pads, and detergent | Usually medium-high after acclimation | Useful but expensive second-stage upgrade |
| **High-velocity dryer** | Shernbao single-/dual-motor models | ¥400–1,200 | Normally manual airflow and heat controls | Clean filter; inspect hose and nozzle; requires storage and active handling | Low-medium because of sound and air pressure | Buy only for frequent home bathing |

## Best choice: grooming vacuum

A Samoyed’s shedding originates on the dog. Removing loose undercoat during grooming reduces how much reaches floors, furniture, and clothing; a robot only collects hair after it has spread through the home.

Oneisall describes its grooming vacuums as brush/trim/vacuum systems designed for long and double coats. It positions the **LM2 as the value model** and the **BM3 as the higher-suction, larger-bin model for heavy shedders**. The company also acknowledges that acceptance varies and emphasizes quieter operation and gradual adjustment. [citation:Oneisall Pet Grooming Vacuum](https://oneisall.com/pages/pet-grooming-vacuum)

### Which model to choose

- **Buy Oneisall LM2** if it is at least ¥200 cheaper than BM3.
- **Buy BM3** if the price difference is small and its larger cup is confirmed by the authorized China seller.
- Neakasa is a reasonable alternative when local warranty service and replacement filters are easier to obtain.
- Choose a model with:
  - A large dust cup
  - Adjustable suction
  - Metal-pin slicker or de-shedding attachment
  - Washable or readily available filters
  - At least a one-year domestic warranty
  - A low setting suitable for acclimation

### Samoyed-specific precautions

- Use the machine mainly to **brush and collect loose coat**.
- Do not shave Xiao Bai’s body coat for convenience. Reserve clipping attachments for paw-pad or sanitary work only if the family knows how to use them safely.
- Start with the machine several metres away, reward calm behaviour, then introduce the hose and brush with suction off before using the lowest suction.
- Stop if the skin becomes red or Xiao Bai repeatedly avoids, freezes, pants heavily, or tries to escape.
- During seasonal shedding, short, frequent sessions are usually easier to accept than one long session.

## When a robot becomes worthwhile

For a spacious home, a robot can save substantial daily labour after the grooming vacuum has reduced the hair load. Roborock’s P20 Pro is particularly relevant because it combines dual rollers and an anti-tangling side brush with a claimed 18,500 Pa suction system. A reported 11.11 subsidized price was approximately **¥2,265 for the water-tank version**, versus a ¥3,999 launch price; that promotional price should be considered a purchase threshold, not a permanently available price. [citation:Roborock P20 Pro pricing and specifications](https://www.ithome.com/0/889/634.htm)

Dreame and ECOVACS are credible alternatives. Current Dreame systems emphasize mapping, obstacle recognition, automatic dust collection, mop washing, and drying, but these conveniences also increase the number of consumable and maintenance components. [citation:Dreame China product overview](https://dreametechcn.com/)

**Recommended robot rule:** buy a Roborock P20 Pro or comparable Dreame model only when the final price is around **¥2,500 or less**, replacement parts are inexpensive, and the home layout is robot-friendly. Avoid paying flagship prices merely for voice control or AI features.

A robot should not run unsupervised until the family is confident it can recognize or avoid pet waste, water bowls, cables, toys, and long curtains.

## When the dryer is worthwhile

A Samoyed’s dense coat must be dried thoroughly after bathing, so a high-velocity dryer can be useful. Nevertheless, it is a relatively noisy single-purpose device. For a student-budget household:

- Buy one only if Xiao Bai is bathed at home approximately monthly or more often.
- Choose an established Shernbao model with adjustable airflow, a grounded 220 V plug, overheat protection, and domestic warranty.
- A single-motor model is normally the value choice; dual-motor models are faster but louder and more expensive.
- Do not use maximum heat or point the nozzle continuously at the face, ears, genitals, or one patch of skin.
- If home bathing is infrequent, occasional professional drying is financially more sensible than storing another large appliance.

# 2. Food comparison

## Mainstream options

| Brand family | Relative cost | Quality-control/formulation confidence | Palatability | Ongoing cost | Appropriate role |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| **Purina Pro Plan** | Medium-high | Strong manufacturer nutrition and formulation resources | Generally high | Usually better value than premium therapeutic-positioned ranges | **Preferred value/reliability candidate** |
| **Royal Canin Medium/Maxi** | High | Strong formulation, breed/size and veterinary range support | Generally high | Higher price per day | Good if Xiao Bai already thrives on it or needs a specific formulation |
| **Hill’s Science Diet** | High | Strong nutrition and veterinary positioning | Medium-high | High, with less consistent China availability by SKU | Suitable alternative if locally available and vet-supported |
| **NatureBridge/比瑞吉 or Bernard/伯纳天纯 complete diets** | Medium | Established domestic availability; evidence transparency varies by line | Often high | Lower than many imported brands | Budget alternative after label and manufacturer checks |
| **Very cheap marketplace or “fresh-meat percentage” brands** | Low | Highly variable | Often high because of coatings/flavourings | Low initially | Avoid unless manufacturer, complete-diet status, traceability, and testing are clear |

## Recommended approach

1. **If Xiao Bai is healthy and doing well on the current food, do not switch simply because another package looks more premium.**
2. If a switch is needed, begin by comparing:
   - Purina Pro Plan adult formula for Xiao Bai’s verified adult size
   - Royal Canin Medium Adult or Maxi Adult, selected by verified adult weight
   - One established domestic complete adult food as a lower-cost comparison
3. Compare **cost per day**, not bag price:
   - `Daily cost = bag price ÷ bag weight × prescribed grams per day`
4. Buy a small bag first. Transition over approximately 7–10 days and monitor stool, vomiting, itching, ears, appetite, body weight, and coat.
5. Treats and food placed in toys should come from the daily calorie allowance rather than being added without adjustment.

For budgeting only, a healthy adult Samoyed might consume roughly one large bag every four to six weeks, but the actual amount must come from the food’s energy density, Xiao Bai’s measured weight, activity, and body condition. Do not set the feeder from breed name alone.

## Recommendation: Mijia Smart Pet Feeder 2

The feeder has a **5 L hopper holding up to approximately 2.5 kg**, a removable 304 stainless-steel bowl, integrated weight sensing, food-level and jam alerts, app scheduling, and dry-battery backup. It requires 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi for app connection. A user test also reported jamming when oversized dog biscuits were used, which is particularly relevant for large-dog kibble. [citation:Mijia Smart Pet Feeder 2 review](https://zhongce.sina.com.cn/article/view/178784)

**Target price:** approximately **¥300–450**.

Before committing:

- Measure several pieces of Xiao Bai’s normal kibble.
- Buy from an authorized seller with a return window.
- Run at least 30–50 test portions.
- Weigh the output rather than assuming each app “portion” equals a precise nutritional serving.
- Confirm that saved schedules continue during internet loss.
- Install fresh backup batteries and test power-loss dispensing.
- Wash the stainless bowl daily; clean and fully dry the hopper and dispensing rotor regularly.

For a Samoyed, 2.5 kg is not a long-duration supply. The feeder is useful for punctual feeding and remote verification, but it should **not** replace a family member checking Xiao Bai every day. Keep a plain stainless-steel bowl available if the feeder jams or loses power.

I would skip camera-equipped feeders: a separate pan/tilt camera provides a much wider view and is cheaper to replace independently.

The newly released Mijia Smart Pet Fountain 2 disinfection version was listed at **¥199** with a 3 L reservoir, washable IPX7 construction, dry-run protection, approximately 30 dB operation, battery operation, filter reminders, and three flow modes. Its advertised 15-day capacity refers to one cat—not a Samoyed. [citation:Mijia Smart Pet Fountain 2 disinfection version](https://www.ithome.com/0/993/766.htm)

## Recommendation

Buy the **Mijia 3 L fountain only together with a wide 2–3 L 304 stainless-steel backup bowl**. This combination is cheaper and safer than depending on one expensive large fountain.

Operating routine:

- Refill and visually check it at least daily.
- Replace all water and wash the drinking surfaces frequently; filtration is not a substitute for cleaning.
- Disassemble and clean the pump/impeller weekly or according to observed slime and hair accumulation.
- Replace filters based on actual condition and manufacturer instructions, not only an app countdown.
- Use filtered Shanghai tap water if the family already filters its own drinking water.
- Never leave Xiao Bai with only an electric fountain during a long absence.

If Xiao Bai refuses moving water, return the fountain and use two large stainless-steel bowls. Pet acceptance is more important than smart features.

## Recommendation

Choose **one Xiaomi C500 Pro/C700-class or EZVIZ C6-series indoor camera**, whichever offers the better authorized-store price, warranty, physical privacy control, and microSD recording at checkout.

For a spacious home, position it to cover:

- Xiao Bai’s sleeping/resting area
- Feeder and water station
- The primary entrance or room transition

Avoid relying on treat-tossing cameras: they cost more, can reinforce barking or demanding behaviour, and provide little extra practical value when the family already has a smart feeder.

Privacy setup:

- Use local microSD recording instead of a cloud subscription where practical.
- Create a family-sharing account rather than sharing the owner password.
- Enable two-factor authentication if offered.
- Install firmware updates.
- Disable unnecessary microphones, cloud recording, or remote-access features.
- Do not point the camera at private household spaces.
- Because you live in Beijing while the dog is in Shanghai, designate one family member as the person responsible for responding to alerts; remote viewing is not physical care.

No toy is indestructible, so inspect it after every session and remove it if chunks, cracks, loose rope, exposed stuffing, or a damaged squeaker appear.

# Purchase and acceptance plan

1. **Confirm Xiao Bai’s age and weight before food and toy sizing.**
2. Photograph the current food label and ask whether Xiao Bai is thriving on it.
3. Buy the grooming vacuum from an authorized seller with a seven-day return option.
4. Introduce each noisy or moving device separately over several days.
5. Test the feeder for one week while someone is physically present.
6. Keep backup food and water bowls permanently available.
7. Start the fountain beside the existing bowl; do not remove the old bowl until Xiao Bai drinks reliably.
8. Run the robot, if purchased later, only under supervision at first.
9. Use the camera for welfare checks—not as a replacement for walking, interaction, water inspection, and daily human care.
10. Reassess after one month: keep products that reduce work without reducing Xiao Bai’s comfort, and return or stop using anything he persistently avoids.

## Final recommendation

The strongest cost-effective combination is:

**Oneisall LM2/BM3 grooming vacuum + a verified complete adult food + Mijia Smart Pet Feeder 2 + Mijia 3 L fountain with a large stainless backup bowl + Xiaomi/EZVIZ local-storage camera + KONG and washable foraging toy.**

This concentrates spending on direct shedding control, reliable feeding, water redundancy, and remote visibility. The robot vacuum is a valuable second purchase for a spacious home, but it is not the best first use of limited student savings. The dryer is conditional on frequent home bathing.
