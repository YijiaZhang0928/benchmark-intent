# Recommended pet-care setup for Xiao Bai

## Bottom line

For one Samoyed living with family in a spacious Shanghai home, I would **not maximize automation in every category**. The best-value setup is:

1. **Cleaning:** a good cordless vacuum first; add a robot only if the family wants daily automated floor cleaning.
2. **Food:** keep Xiao Bai’s current complete diet if it is working well; if changing, trial **Purina Pro Plan Large Breed** or **Royal Canin Maxi Adult/GR26** after confirming age and weight.
3. **Feeding:** a large 304-stainless-steel bowl as the primary feeder, plus a **Mijia 5 L smart feeder** as a scheduling and backup tool.
4. **Water:** a serviceable **Homerunpet 3 L stainless-steel fountain**, but always retain a large stainless-steel water bowl.
5. **Camera:** a **Xiaomi/Mijia or TP-Link pan-and-tilt camera with microSD storage**, rather than a subscription-oriented pet camera.
6. **Toys:** **KONG Extreme**, a correctly sized **Chuckit! Ultra Ball**, and one supervised enrichment toy.

Estimated initial spend, excluding food:

- **Practical cordless-vacuum bundle:** approximately **¥2,500–4,000**
- **Robot-vacuum bundle:** approximately **¥4,000–6,500**
- Estimated non-food recurring cost: roughly **¥40–120/month**
- Food will probably be the dominant ongoing expense, but a responsible monthly estimate requires Xiao Bai’s actual weight, life stage, body condition, and daily ration.

Prices below are **planning ranges rather than guaranteed quotations**. Chinese e-commerce prices fluctuate heavily with coupons, livestream discounts, 618/Double 11 campaigns, and model replacement.

# 2. Recommended combination

## A. Cleaning system: start with a cordless vacuum

### Recommended

**Dreame cordless vacuum in the current ¥1,000–1,800 range**, chosen from its official flagship store, plus:

- Washable rubber pet-hair brush or floor squeegee
- Enzyme-based pet accident cleaner
- Microfiber mop
- Small grooming-area mat

### Why this is the best starting point

A Samoyed’s hair appears not just on open floors but also along skirting boards, under furniture, on sofas, and around the feeding area. A robot helps with daily floor coverage but does not replace targeted cleaning.

A cordless vacuum:

- Costs less initially
- Has no self-empty bags
- Is easier to repair or replace
- Handles furniture and corners
- Can be used immediately when shedding clusters appear
- Is less likely to frighten the dog than a machine moving around autonomously

Choose a model with:

- Detachable, washable cyclone and filter
- Motorized floor head with a removable roller
- Anti-tangle comb or easy-access roller
- Official replacement battery availability
- A wall or floor stand that suits the minimalist home
- No requirement for proprietary cleaning liquid

### Robot upgrade

If Xiao Bai’s family wants automatic daily floor cleaning, buy a **mature, discounted Roborock or Dreame model one generation below the flagship**, rather than paying for the newest launch.

Minimum specifications:

- Anti-tangle main brush
- Self-empty dock
- Object recognition
- Washable mop system
- Room-specific scheduling
- Replaceable rather than permanently enclosed components
- Official Shanghai service coverage

Budget approximately **¥2,500–4,000**. A Roborock model is preferable if the price difference from the comparable Dreame is less than about ¥500; otherwise, Dreame is the value choice.

### Important dog-specific limitation

Do not rely on obstacle detection to identify every urine, vomit, or fecal accident. A robot can spread an accident across the floor. Have a family member visually check the floor before starting it remotely.

Run the robot initially while Xiao Bai is outside or in another room. Introduce it at low power and never allow chasing or biting of the robot.

## B. Food: do not change solely to “upgrade” the brand

### First choice

If Xiao Bai currently has:

- Stable weight and normal body condition
- Formed stools
- Healthy skin and coat
- No persistent itching or gastrointestinal problems
- Good willingness to eat

then **continue the current complete dog food** unless a veterinarian identifies a reason to switch. Changing food creates more acceptance and digestive risk than retaining a diet that already works.

### If a change is needed

Shortlist:

1. **Purina Pro Plan Large Breed adult formula** — best balance of established manufacturer and cost.
2. **Royal Canin Maxi Adult/GR26** — typically very palatable and easy to source, but often slightly more expensive.
3. **Hill’s large-breed adult formula** — credible alternative, but less attractive under a student budget.

Royal Canin officially organizes its nutrition by variables including size, breed, age, and lifestyle, which is why the exact SKU should not be chosen until Xiao Bai’s age and weight are known [citation:Royal Canin China](https://www.royalcanin.com.cn/).

### Food-safety purchasing rules

Buy only a product that:

- Is labeled as a **complete/full-price diet** for the correct life stage
- Has a clear manufacturer, production site, lot number, and expiration date
- Comes from an official flagship or authorized retailer
- Has an intact original bag and reliable seal
- Provides calorie or feeding information
- Can be purchased consistently rather than only through sporadic parallel imports

Avoid selecting food merely because it advertises:

- “Grain-free”
- Very high crude protein
- A high fresh-meat percentage
- Freeze-dried pieces
- “Human-grade” ingredients
- Breed-specific marketing without meaningful nutritional justification

### Trial procedure

Buy the smallest practical bag first. If switching, transition over approximately a week or longer:

- Days 1–2: mostly old food
- Days 3–4: roughly half old and half new
- Days 5–6: mostly new
- Then fully new if stools and appetite remain normal

Weigh portions rather than relying on “cups.” Obtain Xiao Bai’s weight and body-condition assessment before setting the daily ration.

## C. Feeder: Mijia smart feeder plus a conventional bowl

### Recommended combination

- **Mijia Smart Pet Food Feeder 2, approximately 5 L class**
- **Separate wide 304-stainless-steel bowl**, around 1.5–2 L
- Airtight food storage bin kept indoors

Target purchase ceiling: **about ¥600 for the smart feeder**, excluding the separate bowl.

### Why Mijia rather than PETKIT

Mijia should provide enough automation for this household:

- Scheduled meals
- Portion programming
- Low-food or feed-status notifications
- Widely used app ecosystem
- Lower acquisition cost
- Fewer premium analytics Xiao Bai’s family probably will not use

PETKIT is worthwhile if detailed eating records, integrated hydration data, or a feeder camera is a priority. In this case, a separate home camera offers better room coverage and avoids paying twice for cameras.

### Limitations for a Samoyed

A 5 L hopper is not a long-vacation solution for a larger dog. Its actual duration depends on kibble density and Xiao Bai’s ration. It should be treated as:

- A meal-timing aid
- Protection against a temporarily delayed caregiver
- A way for you to monitor routine from Beijing

It should **not** replace family verification that Xiao Bai has eaten.

Before relying on programmed portions, dispense ten portions into a kitchen scale and calculate the average grams per portion. Kibble shape and density can change dispensing accuracy.

Wash the stainless bowl daily and the dispensing chute weekly. Do not continually top up old kibble; empty and wipe the hopper before refilling.

## D. Water: Homerunpet stainless fountain plus backup bowl

### Recommended

- **Homerunpet/霍曼 current 3 L stainless-steel-contact fountain**
- **One separate 2–3 L 304-stainless-steel water bowl**

Expected combined cost: approximately **¥350–650**.

### Why this beats a premium smart fountain

A Samoyed needs reliable access to water more than app-based drinking analytics. The main priorities should be:

- Easy-to-remove water-contact parts
- Stainless-steel drinking surface
- Pump that can be opened for cleaning
- Readily available filters
- Low noise
- No narrow corners that trap biofilm
- Simple refill procedure for Xiao Bai’s family

PETKIT’s premium EverSweet Max 2 advertises a 3 L capacity, app drinking tracking, and long cordless operation, but those features add complexity and filter dependence [citation:PETKIT Smart Pet-Care Ecosystem](https://www.petkit.com/). They are not necessary for this cost-conscious household.

### Fountain acceptance and safety

Some dogs readily accept flowing water; others dislike pump noise or the unfamiliar surface. For the first two weeks:

1. Place the fountain beside Xiao Bai’s usual water bowl.
2. Run it intermittently at first.
3. Do not remove the conventional bowl until regular fountain use is confirmed.
4. Even after acceptance, retain the bowl as protection against power, pump, filter, or app failure.

Rinse the drinking surface daily. Fully dismantle the fountain and clean the pump approximately weekly; Shanghai’s water conditions may require more frequent descaling. Replace filters according to visible contamination and manufacturer guidance rather than blindly subscribing to an aggressive replacement schedule.

## E. Camera: Xiaomi/Mijia with local storage

### Recommended

Choose a current **Xiaomi/Mijia 3K-class pan-and-tilt indoor camera** with:

- microSD local storage
- Two-way audio
- Night vision
- Motion notifications
- Adjustable detection zone
- A privacy or sleep mode
- Shared family access

Planning price: **¥200–450**, plus **¥50–100** for a high-endurance microSD card.

A comparable TP-Link pan-and-tilt camera is the alternative if the family prefers its app or network equipment.

### Why not Furbo

Furbo has pet-specific interaction and smart alerts, which could be attractive because you study in Beijing. However, its official Mini 360 listing was ¥799, and the page promotes additional “pet nanny” functionality [citation:Furbo Mini 360 China Product Page](https://furbo.com/cn/products/furbo-mini-360). For your priorities, the extra cost and potential service-plan dependence outweigh specialized pet recognition.

Use the money saved on:

- Better food
- Replacement filters
- A more durable toy
- A higher-endurance storage card
- A cleaning upgrade

### Placement and privacy

Mount the camera high enough that Xiao Bai cannot reach its cable. Aim it at the main rest and feeding area rather than covering private family spaces.

Obtain agreement from everyone living in the Shanghai home. Use a separate strong password, enable account security options, keep firmware updated, and disable unnecessary cloud recording. Avoid frequently speaking through the camera: a remote voice with no visible person can confuse or agitate some dogs.

## F. Toys: a three-function set, not a large collection

### Recommended starter set

1. **KONG Extreme XL or appropriately weight-rated size** — approximately ¥120–220
   For food stuffing, chewing, and independent enrichment.

2. **Chuckit! Ultra Ball in a size Xiao Bai cannot swallow** — approximately ¥70–140
   For outdoor fetch. Do not use an undersized tennis ball.

3. **Washable sniffing or puzzle toy from GiGwi or another traceable brand** — approximately ¥60–150
   For supervised mental enrichment.

### Why these types

The combination covers three different needs:

- Chewing and settling
- Interactive exercise
- Scent-based mental work

More toys do not necessarily improve acceptance. Rotate two or three toys every few days so they retain novelty.

### Safety rules

Exact size cannot be selected until Xiao Bai’s weight, mouth size, and chewing style are known. Purchase the larger size if between two manufacturer size ranges.

A toy should not:

- Fit completely behind the canine teeth
- Shed chunks during chewing
- Have easily detached eyes, ribbons, bells, or squeakers
- Be so hard that it cannot be indented at all
- Be left unsupervised until Xiao Bai’s chewing behavior is known

Discard toys when they crack, split, become small enough to swallow, or expose internal parts.

# 4. Purchase order

To avoid wasting limited savings, purchase in this order:

1. **Confirm age, current weight, body condition, current food, and chewing style.**
2. Buy the **stainless bowls, camera, and durable toys**.
3. Add the **cordless vacuum** if the family does not already own a good one.
4. Introduce the **fountain alongside the old water bowl**.
5. Add the **smart feeder**, but keep family verification.
6. Consider a **robot vacuum only after one or two months**, once the family can judge whether manual hair cleaning is still burdensome.

This order prioritizes products with immediate, dependable value and delays the most expensive purchase until its utility is demonstrated.

# 5. Final checkout checklist

Use official mainland flagship stores or authorized dealers and verify:

- Chinese invoice availability
- Shanghai warranty and repair handling
- Return period after opening
- Cost and availability of filters, pumps, batteries, rollers, and bowls
- Food lot number and expiration date
- 304-stainless-steel designation for food/water contact parts
- Whether app features work in mainland China
- Whether cloud service is optional
- Whether the camera supports local microSD recording
- Whether the feeder’s kibble-size range matches Xiao Bai’s food
- Whether toy sizing explicitly covers Xiao Bai’s eventual measured weight

## Final recommendation in one line

**Buy a Dreame cordless vacuum, retain or carefully validate Xiao Bai’s current complete food, use a Mijia 5 L feeder with a large stainless bowl, a Homerunpet stainless fountain with a backup bowl, a locally stored Xiaomi camera, and a KONG/Chuckit!/sniffing-toy set; postpone the robot vacuum and avoid subscription-heavy pet cameras.**

Official product and brand pages were prioritized. Product acceptance also cannot be predicted from brand alone; the staged introduction procedures above are therefore part of the recommendation.
