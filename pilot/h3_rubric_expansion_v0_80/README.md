# H3 task-specific rubric expansion v0.80

## Purpose

This package records the measurement repair requested after the first-three-task pilot showed a severe ceiling effect. It does not contain new model outputs and does not claim that H3 has been validated.

The user-facing workbook is generated from `0912_pdr_bench_15_reselected_clarification_gt_with_average_preferences.xlsx`. The original instruction, persona fields, clarification preferences, and legacy direction rubrics are preserved. The legacy five-item `0/1/2` score is explicitly relabelled as a direction diagnostic rather than a P-score.

## Rubric sets

- `P_official`: unchanged released PDR-Bench criteria, criterion weights, dimension weights, evaluator prompt, and calculator. It is available only when the workbook's exact source task–persona pair is present in the released criteria file.
- `P_strict`: a project-specific sensitivity score available for all 15 workbook tasks. Each task has 26 atomic leaves: 5 GOAL, 8 CONT, 5 PRES, and 8 ACTI. High-impact preferences receive four consequential leaves; average-impact preferences receive two.
- `P_HI`: the high-impact subset of `P_strict`. It requires a traceable counterfactual link from the user value to source routing, shortlist or analysis, recommendation, or risk boundary.
- `Coverage@HighImpact`: a process metric calculated from the clarification conversation. It is never inferred from a good final report and is never substituted for any P score.

The strict anchors are `0/2/4/6/8/10`. Generic best practice is capped at 6–7. A high-impact leaf can receive 8–10 only when the report implements the matched value consequentially and the counterfactual alternative would materially change the deliverable. A score of 10 means full satisfaction of the named score set, not that every high-impact preference was asked or recovered.

## Coverage and provenance

- 15 task-specific `P_strict` bundles
- 26 strict leaves per task; 390 leaves total
- 8 exact source task–persona pairs with released official criteria; 301 official leaves total
- 7 exact pairs without released official criteria: T03, T04, T06, T07, T10, T13, and T15

The released exact-pair counts are T01 34, T02 38, T05 39, T08 40, T09 33, T11 44, T12 35, and T14 38. The other seven pairs must not be reported as official PDR P-scores unless their criteria are generated by the unmodified official pipeline and frozen before model outputs are observed.

All project strict leaves are frozen drafts that require human approval before counted runs. They increase sensitivity, but a harder rubric alone cannot guarantee score separation; the generic, swapped-persona, and matched-persona calibration reports still have to pass the ceiling and sensitivity gates.

## First confirmatory budget assumption

The workbook's budget sheet assumes four model families, four H3 cells, two tasks, and three generation repeats: 96 reports. Generation is budgeted at 50k input plus 8k output tokens per report. Judging is budgeted at three passes per report, 30k input plus 2k output per pass, with a 25% buffer.

At prices checked on 2026-09-12, the recommended initial funding is approximately:

- OpenAI: USD 75, including the fixed OpenAI judge allocation
- Anthropic: USD 15–20
- Google Gemini: USD 10–15
- Moonshot/Kimi: CNY 80–100
- Tavily: USD 30
- Jina: start with the free 10M-token tier; add USD 50 only if needed

These are planning estimates, not spending guarantees. Actual cost depends on cached tokens, context length, search calls, failed runs, and provider price changes. API secrets must stay in the local secret store or environment and must not be committed or pasted into chat.
